FODMAP Label Check

Reads French, German, Italian and English ingredient lists.

How to use this

Tap “Scan a label” to photograph the ingredient list. The first scan downloads the text recogniser, which takes a moment on mobile data; after that it works offline.

Most accurate: the “FODMAP” Shortcut. Tap it (or your Action Button), photograph the ingredient list, and the result opens here automatically. It uses Apple’s own text recognition, which reads small print on shiny packets better than anything in a browser.

Photograph the ingredient list on the back of the pack, not the front. Get close, keep the label flat, and use good light.

Whatever the camera reads appears in the box above. If a word came out wrong, fix it and tap Check again.

This is a shopping aid, not medical advice. Only the Monash FODMAP app has lab-tested serving sizes; everything here is an estimate from reading ingredient names. Amber always means “check the portion in Monash”. Recipes change, so scan the label each time.
