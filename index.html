FODMAP Label Check

Reads French, German, Italian and English ingredient lists.

How to use this

Fastest way: use the “FODMAP” Shortcut. Tap it (or your Action Button), photograph the ingredient list, and the result opens here automatically.

Without the Shortcut: tap the box above, long-press, and choose Scan Text to read the label with the camera.

Photograph the ingredient list on the back of the pack, not the front. Get close, keep the label flat, and use good light.

This is a shopping aid, not medical advice. Only the Monash FODMAP app has lab-tested serving sizes; everything here is an estimate from reading ingredient names. Amber always means “check the portion in Monash”. Recipes change, so scan the label each time.
