FODMAP design system

Component library

Every component in the system, in every state, built out of css/tokens.css and nothing else. The snippet beside each example is the exact string that rendered it, so the two cannot drift.

Open this page over HTTP from the repo root — python3 -m http.server 8000 then http://localhost:8000/docs/design-system.html. Opening the file itself (file://) cannot load the modules that inject gauges, icons and every specimen below.
Light firstAll three references are light. Dark mode is designed, not sampled, and arrives for free through the tokens.
No shadows by default--elevation-0: none on surfaces. Filled buttons get a soft elevation tinted by their fill: grey under white or black, green under lime.
Pills, not rounded rectsButtons, chips, badges and bars are true stadiums. Cards are the only things with a corner radius.
One neutral text rampBlack / #666 / #8E8F91 on every surface. The greenish greys on cream were compression artefacts.
Never a colour literalIf a component needs a colour that is not a token, the token gets added first.
44px and 16pxNothing tappable is under 44px; nothing typeable is under 16px, or iOS Safari zooms in and stays there.
